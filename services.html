Services

We deliver novel and hard-to-access biologics to accelerate drug discovery.

With a proprietary plug-and-play expression platform, ORF Bio develops novel therapeutics from gene to final product, providing services from full concept to commercialization. Our expertise extends to pharmaceutical-scale manufacturing and disruptive, patentable technologies.

Our Specialties

We specialize in growth factors, fusion proteins, and antibodies produced using CHO, HEK, Pichia, Bacillus, and E. Coli. These biologics target high-impact applications such as wound healing, cancer, neuroscience, and immunology, and often aren’t available anywhere else.

End-to-End Services

We manage your molecule across its entire life cycle—from initial construct design through scalable production and delivery.

  • Cell line development: Construct design, clone selection, and stable pool generation optimized for yield and batch-to-batch consistency
  • Upstream/expression: Protein expression across multiple host systems, from small-scale screening to production-scale culture
  • Downstream/purification: Multimode chromatography and purification strategies tuned to your molecule’s profile
  • Analytical and QC: In-process and final-product testing for identity, purity, potency, and stability, with a certificate of analysis on every lot
  • Bulk and delivery: Bulk production and formulation, prepared and shipped to your specifications
  • Tech transfer: Structured process documentation to support downstream development and scale-up

Who We Serve

We partner with industry leaders at every stage—from first prototype to scaled supply. Our partners include protein research innovators, biotech startups, diagnostics developers, academic labs, pharmaceutical companies, and therapeutic developers.
