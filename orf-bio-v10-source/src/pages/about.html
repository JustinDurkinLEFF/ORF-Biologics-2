ORF Bio revolutionizes the journey from molecule design to clinical manufacturing with an unmatched combination of deep expertise, state-of-the-art facilities, and a vertically integrated model.

We design, test, and manufacture advanced biologics and cellular therapies for human and animal health, helping unlock new treatments for traumatic brain injury, rare disease, Alzheimer’s, stroke, and more. By combining drug engineering, disease modeling, and manufacturing in a single integrated platform, we accelerate the development of cutting-edge treatments.

Our approach is based on a hub-and-spoke model for drug development, with a central biologics capability supporting multiple programs. As a result, we transform scientific discovery into scalable therapies faster and more efficiently than traditional biotech models.

Our History

Frontline therapies for many cancers and other diseases have remained stagnant for decades—even though more modern solutions are within reach. Anant Kamath launched ORF Bio in 2024 to fill those gaps and save more lives.

In less than two years of operations, ORF Bio has built partnerships with more than 30 institutions worldwide, including City of Hope, Harvard University, and New York University. Our rare disease program received Institutional Review Board approval in July 2026, and we launched a rare disease registry in MONTH 2026.

Our Leadership

AK

Anant Kamath

CEO

With nearly 40 years of experience in biologics and cellular engineering, Anant Kamath oversees ORF’s production pipeline and works with partners to deliver cutting-edge biologics. His background includes extensive technical operations management and laboratory safety practices, and he has worked alongside some of today’s top researchers. Combining technical operations expertise with a commitment to advancing scalable solutions, he drives innovation in biologics manufacturing.

ST

Sara Ternes

Chief Operating Officer

Sara Ternes leads our research operations, product development, corporate strategy, and external partnerships. With a strong foundation in both scientific innovation and operational leadership, Sara has 10 years of industry experience spanning stem cell biology, disease modeling, bioprocessing, and translational research.

Scientific Advisory Board

With deep expertise across biologics, our scientific advisors provide strategic guidance on emerging trends and help accelerate solutions for drug discovery and biologic development.

MB

Martin M. Bednar, M.D., Ph.D., FAANS

President and CEO, Bednar NeuroRestore LLC and Medical Empowerment LLC

Martin M. Bednar has dedicated more than three decades to pioneering treatments for neurodegenerative and psychiatric diseases. After starting his career as an academic neurosurgeon and conducting research that led to several patents, he transitioned to pharmaceuticals with a role at Pfizer Inc. He has led numerous clinical programs, including creating and executing the first clinical trial for age-related hearing loss, and published more than 90 research articles, book chapters, reviews, and scientific opinion pieces.

MY

Mohammed Yousef, Ph.D.

Principal Consultant, Nofal Scientific Inc.

A biopharmaceutical scientist with more than 25 years of experience in industry and academia, Mohammed Yousef specializes in biomolecular characterization, assay development, and CMC development. Before joining Nofal Scientific, Dr. Yousef worked as a senior manager of method development at CSL Behring, where he led a team in developing analytical methods for plasma-derived therapeutics. He has also worked at Elanco Animal Health, Bio-Rad Laboratories, and the University of Chicago.
