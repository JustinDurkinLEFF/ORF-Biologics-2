Therapeutics

Our therapeutics are paving the way for revolutionary treatments.

Biologics have enormous potential to transform a broad spectrum of diseases and conditions. Produced through advanced recombinant and expression technologies, these versatile molecules can be tailored for use in therapeutic areas such as immunology, oncology, neuroscience, wound healing and rare diseases.

By providing scalable solutions for conditions that currently lack effective therapies, biologics open the door to exciting possibilities for providers and patients.

Therapeutic areas

Our biologics support revolutionary research and therapies for neurodegenerative diseases, brain injuries, and spinal cord regeneration. By offering reproducible biologic formulations and data-driven characterization, we help researchers and clinicians unlock new approaches that save lives.

Using patient-derived induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs), we model rare diseases and evaluate therapeutic candidates in biologically relevant systems. Our platform focuses on areas such as lysosomal storage disorders, rare hematologic diseases, and inherited metabolic conditions.

Our biologics advance personalized cancer therapies and treatments, including antibody drug conjugates and recombinant protein therapeutics. We have deep expertise in immuno-oncology, featuring next-generation antibody drug conjugates and bispecific antibody therapies, enabling targeted, effective treatments with fewer side effects.

We partner with researchers and clinicians to advance wound healing therapies using biologics such as recombinant growth factors, extracellular matrix proteins, and engineered peptide scaffolds.
